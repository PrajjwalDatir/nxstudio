# nxstudio — Founders' Partnership Agreement (Draft)

> **Status: DRAFT for discussion.** This is a plain-language working agreement between
> the two founders, meant to record what was agreed in the 2026-07-29 call. It is not
> legal advice. Before either party relies on it, have it reviewed by a lawyer in a
> relevant jurisdiction — cross-border (India ⇄ Zambia) revenue-sharing and tax
> treatment can differ from what is written here.

## 1. Parties

- **Prajjwal ("Prajj")** — India. Role: **Product.** Owns product development, UX,
  bug-fixing, and the underlying infrastructure of nxstudio.
- **Kema Sambondu ("Kema")** — Zambia. Role: **Distribution.** Owns content creation,
  audience distribution, community/DM support, and go-to-market.

The parties are collaborating as co-founders of **nxstudio** (the "Product"), a build
tool that turns a founder's brief into a derived tech stack and AI build prompts.

## 2. Structure & status

- No company is registered yet. This is a good-faith, trust-based working agreement
  between individuals.
- Product IP, code, domains, and hosting accounts currently sit with Prajj. On
  formation of any legal entity, the parties intend to assign the Product's IP to that
  entity and restate this agreement.
- Either founder may bring in helpers *under themselves* (contractors, editors,
  developers). Each is responsible for managing and paying their own helpers out of
  their own share unless agreed otherwise in writing.

## 3. Revenue split

Net revenue from the Product (subscription income received, after payment-processor
and direct hosting/API costs) is allocated:

| Allocation | Share | Goes to |
|---|---:|---|
| Distribution | **30%** | Kema |
| Product | **30%** | Prajj |
| Operations reserve | **40%** | Held for the Product |

- The **40% operations reserve** is retained to fund the Product: hosting, tooling,
  marketing spend, contractors, and future hires. It is held in Prajj's account on a
  **trust basis** and is **not** personal income to either founder.
- Reserve spending decisions are made jointly. A simple shared ledger (spreadsheet)
  records every reserve inflow and outflow so both founders can see the balance.
- If the reserve is later moved into a company bank account, this clause is superseded
  by the entity's own controls.

## 4. Affiliate / referral program

- Affiliates (including everyday users) earn **20–30%** of the **monthly subscription
  fee** of premium members they refer.
- Payout runs for up to **6 months** per referred subscriber.
- If a referred subscriber **cancels before 6 months**, the affiliate is paid only for
  the months the subscriber actually remained subscribed (pro-rated by month).
- Affiliate payouts are a **cost of the Product** and come out of gross revenue
  **before** the 30/30/40 split is calculated (i.e., both founders share the cost of
  acquisition proportionally).
- Exact percentage within the 20–30% band, and the referral mechanics, are set by the
  referral system and may be tuned jointly based on results.

## 5. Pricing (non-binding, subject to change)

- **Free tier:** one product + basic prompts.
- **Premium (~$50/mo, adjustable):** multiple products + advanced prompt templates
  (security audit, bug hunt, performance, accessibility, deploy).
- Pricing is explicitly **flexible** and will be adjusted based on user feedback by
  joint agreement.

## 6. Responsibilities

**Prajj (Product):** product roadmap, building/maintaining features, UX, output
quality, infrastructure (GitHub sync, `.nx/` standard, billing), the referral system,
the landing site and team page, and keeping the shared reserve ledger.

**Kema (Distribution):** content strategy and production, audience growth, the
"build-what-viewers-request" content loop, community and DM handling, and feeding ICP
insight back into the product roadmap.

## 7. Decisions & communication

- Primary channel: **WhatsApp.**
- Product decisions: Prajj decides, informed by Kema's ICP insight.
- Distribution decisions: Kema decides.
- Pricing, revenue split, reserve spending, hiring, fundraising, and any change to this
  agreement: **joint decision**, recorded in writing (WhatsApp is sufficient).

## 8. Term, exit & IP

- Either founder may exit with **30 days'** written notice.
- On exit, the leaving founder is paid any **already-accrued** share up to the exit
  date (including affiliate obligations already earned), and stops accruing new share
  thereafter.
- Work each founder contributed to the Product before exit remains with the Product.
- Neither founder will, during the partnership and for **12 months** after, launch a
  directly competing product using the other's confidential materials (code, audience
  data, subscriber lists).
- **Confidentiality:** subscriber data, revenue figures, and unreleased product plans
  are confidential and not to be shared outside the partnership.

## 9. Future entity & funding

- The parties intend to bootstrap to consistent revenue before raising external funds.
- Once revenue is consistent, they may jointly pursue accelerators/residencies (e.g.
  Y Combinator) or funding, at which point a formal entity and cap table replace this
  document. Absent a separate written agreement, founder equity in any such entity is
  intended to reflect the spirit of an equal product/distribution partnership — to be
  finalized in writing at formation.
